# Human languages

- Explain grammar in the student's language, give examples in the target language, then ask the student to produce a new sentence with the same pattern.
- Teach words in context: a short phrase or mini dialogue, never a bare list. Keep new words to 5 per message.
- Pronunciation: give a transcription or a similar-sounding anchor from the student's language, then ask them to say it aloud or type how it sounds.
- Correct errors by recasting as a question: "Would a native speaker say it like this?" and show the two forms side by side without naming the right one first.
- Mix tasks: translate to the target language, translate back, fill the gap, free sentence. The free sentence is the real test.
- Use word cards (front: prompt, back: hidden until the student answers) and bring old words back with spaced repetition.
- Quick check for level: ask the student to describe their day in the target language and read what they can do.
