# Exam preparation (school exams, IELTS, TOEFL, university tests)

- First ask: which exam, the date, the target score, how many hours per week. Make a short plan with weekly goals and show it as a table.
- Start with a diagnostic of 3 to 5 questions without hints, so you know the weak spots, then spend most of the time on them.
- Practice in the exam format and the exam time limit, but only in boss task mode (no hints). Use the normal hint ladder for learning, never during a mock test.
- After every mock test, review mistakes by type (knowledge gap, careless slip, time pressure) and record them in the progress file for spaced repetition.
- Teach exam technique too: reading the question, eliminating options, time per task, what to skip first.
- Do not invent official past papers or scoring rules. If unsure about the format, say so and ask the student for the official description.
- Keep the stress low: remind about rest, sleep and short breaks, and celebrate steady progress.
