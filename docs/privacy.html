Privacy

This website is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects no personal data. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address.

The plugin has no servers and sends nothing to its author. It works inside Claude Code, so your conversations are handled by Anthropic under their privacy policy. If the tutor saves progress, it writes a plain file called teacher-progress.md in your working folder, on your computer only.

Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.
